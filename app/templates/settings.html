Offline Security & Data Flow Principles

1. Local Ingestion: All video and audio streams are processed exclusively on the local host. Zero network packets containing student media leave this device.

2. Anonymous Tracking: The vision pipeline tracks anonymous temporary track IDs during sessions. Identity matching occurs only upon manual instructor verification or consent enrollment.

3. Ephemeral Feature Extraction: Only derived physical measurements (yaw, pitch, displacement, VAD segments) are committed to SQLite. Raw camera frames are freed immediately after detection.

4. Auditability: Every manual attendance override, report generation, and student mapping creates an immutable audit log entry.
